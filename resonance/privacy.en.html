Resonance Expedition Offline Edition — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026 · Version 2026-11-11.1 · This English translation is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean original, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information collected automatically during play

"Resonance Expedition Offline Edition" by Banditbul Games (the "Operator") can be played without an internet connection. The Game does not automatically send your nickname or progress to the Operator's servers. App store purchase and settlement processing, and inquiries you send yourself, are covered by sections 6 and 7 below.

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools are used.
  • All fonts, images and sounds are bundled in the app and are not downloaded from outside.
  • App purchases, downloads and updates, sharing services you choose, and processing by the operating system and app store themselves are governed by those services' own policies.

2. Information stored only on your device

ItemsPurposeLocation
Nickname; progress such as heroes, equipment, currencies and stages; loot from interrupted expeditions; last save time; sound, vibration and speed settings; version and time of consent to the termsGame progress, display, and recovery of interrupted rewardsApp storage on your device

"Start over (delete records)" in the Game resets your progress but keeps your nickname and consent record. Clearing app data or deleting the app may erase the information on your device. Backups you export and copies made by your operating system's backup settings are separate; the Operator does not automatically receive or manage them.

3. Backup files

You can export the information in section 2 with "Save file" or "Copy code" in Settings, and restore it with "Load file" or "Load code". In a browser you can download or share the file; in the Android app you can use the share sheet to send the backup to an app you choose. Backups do not include login tokens for online accounts.

You choose where to keep a backup and whom to share it with. If you store or send it via another email, cloud or messaging service, that service's policy applies. The Game does not automatically upload backups to the Operator's servers. Materials you attach to an inquiry yourself are handled under section 7.

4. Device permissions

  • Vibration: used for battle effects on supported devices; can be turned off in Settings.
  • Files, sharing and clipboard: used only to handle the content or file you choose when you run a backup feature. The Game has no feature that reads your contacts, full photo library or location.

5. Children

The Game does not automatically collect children's personal information during play. Inquiries that require personal information of a child under 14 should be submitted through a legal guardian. Purchase eligibility and age ratings follow the app store where you buy the Game.

6. Purchase information

Purchases, payments and refunds are processed by the app store where you bought the Game (Google Play or ONE store). The Game has no payment feature, and the Operator does not directly collect card numbers or payment passwords through the Game.

Google Play's order management, sales and payout reports may provide an order ID, transaction date and time, product/package, device model, amount, currency and tax, payment and refund status, and buyer country. Depending on seller and regional conditions, city, state/province and postal code may be included. The Operator may also check purchase history using an order number or email address that you provide in an inquiry. This is separate from the Game sending play records.

The Operator processes only the data actually provided by the app store, and only for purchase verification, refund support, sales settlement and legal obligations — never for advertising or tracking players. ONE store transactions follow the scope and policies of that store. Retention and deletion of purchase data follow section 8.

About the data Google Play provides: monthly reports, order management.

7. Email inquiries

When you email an inquiry, the Operator processes your sender address, display name, message and any attachments you provide, in order to reply, investigate errors and support purchases/refunds. Please provide your nickname, device/OS information or order number only as needed for the inquiry. Do not send unnecessary information such as national ID numbers, card numbers or passwords.

Inquiries are received at the Gmail address below, and Google's email service is used to deliver and store emails. Transmission by the email service you choose and processing by Google are subject to the Google Privacy Policy, and may be processed and stored outside Korea. The Game does not automatically send inquiry content or device information.

8. Retention and deletion

General inquiry data processed by the Operator is deleted without delay once its purpose is achieved. Where retention is required by law, the necessary data is kept separately from other uses. Under Article 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, records of contracts, withdrawals of offers, payments and supply are kept for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints and dispute handling for 3 years. Where other statutory retention obligations apply, those periods are followed.

When the retention period ends or the data is no longer needed, electronic data is deleted so that it cannot be recovered, and paper records are shredded. Records held by an app store are managed by that store; information on your device under this policy is managed as described in section 2.

9. Your rights and safeguards

You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your information held by the Operator via the contact above, including through a legal guardian or authorized representative. Only the minimum information needed to verify your identity will be requested. Deletion and similar requests may be limited to the extent data must be kept by law, and the reason will be explained.

The Operator limits access to inquiry and transaction data to persons who need it for their work, and applies the measures needed to protect account access and manage data. In principle, data is not provided for other purposes; requests based on law are handled according to that law. Backup sharing you choose yourself is covered by section 3.

Reporting and counseling on privacy violations (Korea): Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 in Korea), Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

10. Changes to this policy

This policy applies from the effective date above and is posted in the app and on a public web page. When the policy version changes in an app update, you will be able to review it again before starting the game. If the purposes or items of processing change, the required notice and consent procedures will be carried out.
